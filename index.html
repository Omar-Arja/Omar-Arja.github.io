Personal Companion

A private AI assistant that helps its owner find information in email and files and review calendar events.

Google connections

With your consent, Personal Companion can search and summarize Gmail messages, find and read selected Google Drive files, and review Google Calendar events. Each account is connected separately. The initial connections request read-only access and do not send email, modify files or edit calendars.

The assistant runs privately. This website provides its application description and privacy policy; visitors cannot access the assistant or connected accounts here.

Google integrations are currently being configured. Account access is activated only after consent and verification of the controls described in the privacy policy.
