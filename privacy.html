Personal Companion

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026. Personal Companion is a private assistant operated for its owner. Google integrations are undergoing setup and must satisfy this policy before activation.

Information accessed

After account consent, the assistant may access your Google account identity, Gmail message content and metadata, Google Drive files and metadata, and Google Calendar events. Only services needed for the connected account are requested. Initial permissions are read-only. A Google permission can cover an entire service; the connector separately controls which resources the assistant may use.

Purpose and AI processing

Information is used to answer your requests, search or summarize messages and files, review your schedule, and carry out tasks you enable. Relevant information may be sent to the configured AI provider, currently OpenAI, to produce those answers. It is not sold, used for advertising or distributed publicly. Sending information to another person requires your instruction.

Google information must not be used to train or improve general-purpose AI models. The assistant's provider settings must support that restriction before Google information is processed. This policy does not promise zero retention by an external provider.

Personal Companion's use of Google Workspace information will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Credentials and private application state are stored on the private server's encrypted storage. Information used in a conversation may appear in private prompts, replies, session records and encrypted recovery copies. Google data is not mirrored into a permanent inbox or Drive database. Connector caching must follow the relevant provider requirements. Retained conversation and recovery records require separate owner management; no automatic deletion or backup schedule is promised.

Your control

You can revoke the application's Google authorization in your Google Account connections. This stops new access. Revocation does not automatically erase information already included in conversation records or recovery copies. Contact the application operator to request removal of retained records; provider-held records remain subject to that provider's controls and policies.

This website

These static pages have no sign-in form, advertising or application analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Connected Google information and assistant conversations are never published on these pages.

Contact and changes

Contact the application operator through the support email shown on the Google consent screen. If connected-data use or permissions change, this policy will be updated and any required new consent will be obtained before the change takes effect.
